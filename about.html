Our Story

About Recipe Secrets

A decade of seasonal cooking, warm hospitality and honest flavour.

Where We Began

A Kitchen Built on Family Recipes

Recipe Secrets opened its doors in 2013 with a simple idea: cook the dishes our families taught us, using the best ingredients we could find, and serve them to our neighbours like honoured guests.

What started as a 12-seat dining room has grown into one of the city's most-loved tables, but the heart of it hasn't changed one bit.

Our Craft

Technique Meets Intuition

Our kitchen blends classical French technique with the produce of our region. Sauces are built slowly. Bread is baked fresh each morning. Nothing leaves the pass unless our chefs would serve it to their own families.

We change our menu with the seasons, so what you taste in spring won't be what you taste in autumn — and that's exactly how we like it.

The Room

A Space Designed for Lingering

Candlelight, reclaimed wood and soft jazz set the tone for long, unhurried dinners. Whether it's date night or a long-overdue reunion with friends, we want you to stay a while.
